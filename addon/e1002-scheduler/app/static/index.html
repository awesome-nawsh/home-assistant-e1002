E1002 Screens

Device

The display wakes on its own schedule — there is no way to wake a deep-sleeping device over Wi-Fi. For an immediate refresh press the green button on the unit.

Screens

Schedules may not overlap — at most one screen is active at any moment. Outside all windows the device sleeps until the next one (max 6 h).
