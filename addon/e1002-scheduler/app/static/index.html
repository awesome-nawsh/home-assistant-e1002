E1002 Screens

Device

The display wakes on its own schedule — there is no way to wake a deep-sleeping device over Wi-Fi. For an immediate refresh press the green button on the unit.

Screens

Screens whose windows overlap rotate in list order, each staying up for its dwell time (2–60 min). Outside all windows the panel is blanked (or shows the fallback) and the device sleeps until the next window (6 h hops).
